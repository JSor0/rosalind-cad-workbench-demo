# Claims and caveats

**Supported:** X5S1 is a highly divergent, structurally remodeled CAD/DFF40-family candidate. Fresh whole-domain searches, a reciprocal search, family context and selected conserved positions support that assignment. Its associated locus is present in deposited genomic records and expressed in the six pooled libraries.

**Not established:** nuclease activity, DNA binding, metal occupancy, ICAD independence, protective function during desiccation, a particular evolutionary loss event, or definitive deep phylogenetic placement.

- X5S1 is not the only significant hit below 20% identity: the other is annotated R. varieornatus DFF40/VEE8. X5S1 is the only such hit lacking a CAD/DFF40 label in the retrieved subset.
- Exact accession absence in the clustered forward database may reflect clustering.
- These two-direction searches are not a reciprocal-best-hit pair: forward X5S1 rank 290, reciprocal mouse rank 1.
- Foldseek 3Di+AA is structure-guided, not sequence-independent.
- The sequence-negative statement is restricted to the executed direct MMseqs2 search and parameters.
- The 7.22-fold expression difference is descriptive locus-level context, not a significance result or a mechanism.
- Local phylogenetic sensitivity work remains preserved in the broader project, outside this compact review repository. The included FoldMason guide tree must not be presented as that inference.

No new biological results were computed to prepare this handoff. File/row counts, checksums and canonical-value assertions are packaging QA only.
