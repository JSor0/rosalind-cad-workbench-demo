# Review and data notes

This repository is a curated review copy of Jan Soroczynski's Rosalind Workbench competition project, prepared for public sharing with his authorization. No project-wide open-source license is granted here. Inclusion does not relicense third-party material.

Experimental coordinates originate from [PDB 1V0D](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1V0D). Existing predicted monomer structures originate from [AlphaFold DB](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/); sequences and annotations from [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/) and associated public databases; genomic records from NCBI; processed expression from [GEO GSE94295](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE94295), GSM2472501–GSM2472506. These data retain their source terms and attribution. Exact sequence/coordinate/matrix bytes, accessions, versions and hashes are retained where included. Six small deposited processed matrices and the frozen proteome FASTA are included to make the supporting calculations inspectable offline; no raw reads are included.

Figures and animation were produced under Jan's scientific direction from recorded data and real molecular coordinates. The approved mouse and tardigrade artwork was supplied by Jan and serves only as presentation artwork, not anatomical evidence. The main images and GIF remain unchanged from the current reviewed package.

The archived v2.3.2 viewer bundles Mol* and gifenc, whose upstream MIT terms apply to those components. Its full HTML, bundled runtimes, unrelated dimer–DNA contexts and third-party documents are excluded. Source location and immutable artifact hash are recorded in [the provenance ledger](provenance/PROVENANCE_LEDGER.md). Composition can be reproduced from included saved render assets; a full molecular rerender requires separate access to that archive and its notices.

No third-party endorsement is implied. Public availability does not change the source terms or grant additional reuse rights. A computational family hypothesis does not establish biochemical activity.
